A growing number of people, both teenagers and adults, are not getting the amount of sleep they need to stay healthy. Research from the Sleep Health Foundation and Psychology Today shows that around 39.8% of Australian adults experience inadequate sleep. As for teenagers, 75% of teens reported getting less sleep than 8 hours due to school pressure, irregular routines, and late-night technology use.

Busy schedules, school stress, work demands, and constant use of technology all contribute to poor sleep habits. Even though sleep is essential for brain function, memory, mood, and physical health, many individuals don't realise how little they are actually sleeping or how it affects their daily lives. 

Because of this, I believe there is a need to create a simple and accessible software tool that can help individuals monitor their sleep and become more aware of their habits. By tracking sleep patterns and providing insights this project can help users recognise unhealthy routines and encourage them to make a change that can support their wellbeing.

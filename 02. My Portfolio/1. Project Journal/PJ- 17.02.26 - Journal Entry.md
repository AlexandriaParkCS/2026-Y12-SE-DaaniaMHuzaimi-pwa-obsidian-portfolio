**Date:** 17 February  
**Project:** Sleep Tracker PWA  
**Module:** Problem Description and Proposed Solution

**What I did:**
- **PP-02 Problem Description:** While writing my problem description, I researched my target market, which I chose to be people who find it difficult to sleep.
- **PP-03 Proposed Solution:** I researched possible solutions to the problem and decided to create a sleep tracker that allows users to input their sleeping times, which then calculates how long they slept for.
- I chose the name **“SleepTracker”** for my PWA because I could not think of a more creative name.

 **Why I did it:**
- For Assessment Task 2, students are required to create a PWA for a targeted audience, which is why I chose people who experience sleeping difficulties.
- As a teenager myself, I sometimes struggle with sleeping. Since I did not always know the reason why, I thought it would be useful to create a PWA that allows users to track their sleep and become more aware of their sleeping habits.
- I chose the name **“SleepTracker”** because I was unsure what to call the application, and since its main purpose is to track sleep, I felt the name suited it well.

 **Problems:**
- Identifying the exact cause of the problem took some time, as it was difficult to determine the specific issues affecting my target audience.
- One problem I encountered during this process was deciding what I needed to create in terms of identifying my target market, understanding their problem, and finding a suitable solution.
- I also struggled to come up with a name for the application, so I decided to keep it simple and call it **“SleepTracker.”**
